# Weekly Project Update Meeting Proposal

## 1. Purpose
- Ensure the entire software team maintains a shared, current understanding of project scope, priorities, risks, and dependencies.
- Surface blockers early so they can be resolved before they impact delivery dates.
- Keep stakeholders informed and engaged without relying on ad-hoc status pings.

## 2. Cadence & Logistics
- **Frequency:** Every Wednesday.
- **Time:** 10:00–10:45 local time to balance overlap across time zones.
- **Duration:** 45 minutes, capped with timer prompts at 30 and 40 minutes.
- **Format:** Hybrid video conference with an always-available link; in-room whiteboard for co-located members.
- **Facilitation:** Rotating Scrum Master or Project Lead keeps the meeting on agenda and records outcomes.

## 3. Participants & Roles
- **Core engineering team:** Share progress, demo increments, raise blockers.
- **Product manager:** Clarify priorities, note scope adjustments.
- **Design & QA representatives:** Flag UX or quality issues and upcoming validation needs.
- **Key stakeholders (optional):** Attend once per month for broader alignment.

## 4. Standard Agenda (45 min)
1. **Opening (5 min):** Quick wins, reminder of sprint/iteration goals, review action items from prior week.
2. **Team Round-Robin (20 min):** Each squad covers accomplishments, upcoming work, risks, or dependencies (2 minutes per squad).
3. **Deep Dive Topic (10 min):** Focus on the highest-risk initiative, architecture change, or customer escalation.
4. **Metrics & Delivery Health (5 min):** Review burndown, deployment cadence, defect trend, and SLA indicators.
5. **Decisions & Action Items (5 min):** Summarize next steps, owners, and due dates. Confirm notes distribution plan.

## 5. Preparation Requirements
- Update tickets and dashboards by end of day Tuesday so data reflects reality.
- Add proposed deep-dive topics to the shared agenda doc 24 hours in advance; facilitator prioritizes based on urgency.
- Pre-read materials (design mocks, architecture diagrams, risk reports) linked in the agenda to save time on screen-sharing.

## 6. Collaboration Tooling
- **Agenda & Notes:** Shared document (Confluence/Notion) with persistent sections for agenda, decisions, and parking lot.
- **Live Status:** Project board plus burn-up/burndown charts auto-refreshed from the ALM tool.
- **Action Tracking:** Use the same issue tracker with a dedicated “Meeting Action” label for transparency.
- **Recording:** Enable transcription + recording for asynchronous team members.

## 7. Follow-Up & Accountability
- Notes and action items sent within 4 business hours; template includes owner, due date, and status.
- Outstanding actions reviewed at the start of the next meeting until closed.
- Risks escalated during the meeting are logged in the centralized risk register with mitigation steps and owners.

## 8. Success Metrics & Continuous Improvement
- **Attendance & punctuality:** ≥90% of required members present; late arrivals trend downward over time.
- **Action closure rate:** ≥80% of meeting actions completed before the following session.
- **Cycle-time delta:** Compare average feature cycle time before/after adopting the meeting to validate impact.
- **Feedback loop:** Quarterly retro dedicated to assessing meeting value; adjust cadence, agenda, or tooling accordingly.

## 9. Rollout Plan
- **Week 0:** Circulate proposal, gather feedback, finalize schedule.
- **Week 1:** Pilot session with core team, capture lessons learned.
- **Week 2+:** Standardize format, begin metrics tracking, invite optional stakeholders monthly.
